01 — the measured failure

First, measure the failure.

The Nadaraya-Watson Envelope is a mean-reversion trigger: price pierces a smoothed band, you fade the move. The 12-pair replay shows where it actually fires on 1h: overwhelmingly during strong moves — a hard directional push against a still-flat envelope, the band-walk pattern. The band lags, price walks along it, and the trigger keeps fading a trend that keeps going. Structurally counter-trend exactly when trend is strongest.

Grading the same signals two ways exposes the second failure: a fixed-horizon close judges the endpoint, the triple-barrier judges the path. Path-blind grading flattered the trigger — many "hits" rode through a stop-out first.

⚠ honest caveat — confidence-gate path uses indicator-only confidence (news/research not backtestable); NWE/trend trigger paths are exact.

02 — architecture delta

What changed: gate, don't vote.

Three additions, one demotion. A fourth voter (Derivatives) joins the brain vote; a regime classifier is deliberately not a voter — it gates which triggers are even allowed to emit. Downstream, the grader learns to judge the path and a nightly trainer learns from our own graded history.

03 — emission policy

Gate v2: one truth table.

The whole emission policy fits in six cells. The rule that matters most: a mean-reversion trigger never emits into a classified trend, and 1h — the noisiest timeframe — never emits on confidence alone.

timeframe ● ranging ● mixed ● trending

Every suppression is logged with a reason — the funnel charts in the evidence section are that telemetry, not an estimate.

04 — grading

Grade the path, not the endpoint.

A trade that rides through a −1×ATR stop before recovering was a loss in the real world, whatever the close says k candles later. The triple-barrier method grades what a position would have lived through: take-profit, stop-loss, or time — first touch wins.

Reward map v2 flows only when TB_GRADING_ENABLED

outcomerewardreading
correct+1.0TP first, or timeout with the called direction realized
wrong−4.0SL first — asymmetric pain, as in v1
directional-but-flat−1.5called a move, price went nowhere by the time barrier
missed move−1.0skipped while a real threshold move printed

Edge rules

TP and SL both inside one candle is unknowable from OHLC — graded as pessimistic SL. Barrier never touched ⇒ timeout, labeled by the fixed-horizon rule. Labels are always recorded; only the reward path flips with the flag, and legacy rows keep the old grading path.

06 — self-learning

Learning from our own track record.

Once graded outcomes accumulate, the system stops trusting raw confidence and starts estimating it: a nightly trainer fits a meta-label model and per-timeframe calibration on our own predictions — then runs them in shadow, logged on every row, gating nothing.

M

Meta-label modeltraining/meta_model.py · LogisticRegression

Estimates p(correct | regime, agreement, positioning, confidence, tf, hour, trigger). Requires ≥ 500 graded rows before it writes anything; evaluated on a time-ordered holdout — no shuffle leakage. Logged as meta_p.

08 — honesty

What this deck doesn't claim.

The strongest version of this system is the one whose limits are written down next to its wins.

Confidence path is a proxy

News and research agents call live LLMs and feeds — not replayable. In backtests the confidence gate uses indicator-only confidence. NWE and trend trigger paths are exact; conf_over_80 rows carry that asterisk.

12-pair baseline

Majors + mid-caps, ~254k bars — representative, not exhaustive. The full 48-pair sweep is optional and slow; tail-pair behaviour could shift the small-n groups.

Regime flapping

Hysteresis (enter ADX ≥ 25 or CHOP ≤ 38.2, exit ADX ≤ 20 and CHOP ≥ 45) plus a 2-bar dwell bound the switch rate — but boundary bars are still the noisiest, and they get graded like any other.

Meta can overfit

Guards: ≥ 500 graded rows before training, time-ordered holdout, shadow-first rollout, a single LogReg rather than a model zoo, and per-TF isotonic instead of one global curve.

Frozen stack

The numpy<2 pin (vendored pandas_ta) holds sklearn at 1.3.2. Upgrades are a deliberate migration project, not a pip install — the pins are the price of reproducibility.

News breadth reduced

CryptoPanic's free tier is dead; ingestion is RSS-only until a replacement source is vetted. Fewer headlines in RAG means the news agent sees a narrower world.
