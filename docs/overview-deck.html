03 / The referee

How five opinions become one call

It's weighted voting — and the weights are earned. Louder votes go to whoever has been right more often.

Numbers are each analyst's starting "trust score" — they rise and fall as the system learns.

Each analyst gives a direction + a confidence

Buy, sell, or hold — and how sure it is, from 0 to 100%.

Each has a trust score, learned over time

The Indicator starts most-trusted, then Research, then the rest. These are turned into weights that add up to 100%.

The brain tallies the weighted votes

A confident call from a highly-trusted analyst moves the needle far more than a hesitant vote from a low-trust one.

It only speaks when the room clearly leans

Buy or sell only on a clear lean — otherwise hold. A built-in "dead zone" stops one loud voice from forcing a trade the others disagree with.

Specialists vote, the more-often-right get a louder vote, and it only speaks when the room clearly leans one way.

04 / The "Reinforce" in BitReinforceX

It grades itself — and gets smarter

No human is required in the loop. The system marks its own homework against reality and re-tunes who to trust.

Predict & save

Every call is stored with the exact data each analyst saw, and a "check-back" time.

Wait for reality

Each timeframe has a fair waiting window before judging (a 1-hour call is judged sooner than a daily one).

Check what actually happened

Did the price really move up, down, or stay flat — past a sensible threshold for that timeframe?

Reward or punish

A correct call scores +1. A confidently-wrong call scores −4 — being wrong hurts far more than being right helps, so the system learns caution instead of recklessness.

Everyone learns individually, the brain re-tunes trust

Each analyst adjusts its own judgement, and the brain slowly nudges trust toward whoever's been right lately — a gentle 5% per lesson, so it adapts without whiplash.

A human can override with one tap

The operator can correct any grade from Telegram (✅ / ❌ / ➖ / 🚫), and the math lands exactly where the human says.

This is the moat. The product gets sharper every day on its own — and it publishes its real monthly hit-rate in the channel. Most cheap-signal sellers never dare show their true accuracy. Transparency is the whole marketing angle.

08 / Money in, access out — automatically

How someone pays, and instantly gets in

Two payment rails, both fully automated. No staff, no payment server to babysit. Pay in — you're in. Lapse — you're out. All by itself.

💳 UPI / Cards (Razorpay)

Tap a plan → the bot hands over a payment link (valid 15 minutes) → pay with any UPI app or card → the bot notices within seconds and unlocks access. Cost to us: a ~2% processing fee.

🪙 USDT crypto (TRON network)

The bot asks the buyer to send an exact odd amount — say 7.013 USDT. That tiny tail is a fingerprint that ties one blockchain payment to one buyer, with no account linking. A watcher spots it and unlocks access. Zero fees to us. A "/paid" button force-checks the chain if they're impatient.

Why the odd amount is clever: crypto payments don't carry a name. By asking for 7.013 instead of 7.000, the tiny difference becomes a receipt the bot can match to exactly one person — automatically, for free.

Pay → instant personal invite

A one-use link, valid 48 hours, just for them.

The database is the real gate

Every attempt to join is checked live against active subscriptions — so a leaked or forwarded invite is worthless. Only a paying member gets in.

Auto-remove on lapse
