01 · Pricing philosophy

New entrant. Low friction in, honest product out.

We have no public track record yet — the system's live graded history is still accumulating (statistically meaningful in ~2–3 months). The pricing strategy accepts that: price at impulse-purchase level for India, convert the current testing channel into a visible beta cohort, and let the win-rate history do the marketing later. Raise prices only when the numbers have earned it.

🎯 Impulse-priced entry

₹199 for 7 days is below one food-delivery order. The goal of the 7-day SKU is not profit — it is letting a skeptic verify the signals are real. Profit lives in 30-day renewals and Pro.

📈 Ladder, don't discount

Per-day price falls with commitment (₹28 → ₹23 → ₹20/day). Renewal is always cheaper than churn-and-return — founding subscribers lock their price for 6 months.

🔍 Transparency as moat

Every signal is graded against realized price by the system itself. Publish the monthly hit-rate table in the channel — no competitor in the cheap-signals space dares to.

Anchor rate used across this deck: 1 USDT ≈ ₹88. USDT prices are rounded UP slightly to absorb volatility and network fees — refresh the anchor at launch and re-round.

07 · Payment rails

Two rails, both automatable without a public webhook server

🇮🇳 INR — Razorpay Payment Links

Membership bot creates a Payment Link via Razorpay's REST API with notes={user_id, sku}, DMs it to the buyer (UPI/cards/netbanking), then polls link status every 30s for 15 minutes. Paid → subscription activated. Polling means no inbound port, no webhook infra on the Oracle box.

  • KYC: individual/sole-prop onboarding, ~2% MDR
  • Auto-expire links after 15 min (repricing safety)
  • Refunds scriptable via the same API

₮ USDT — TRC-20 with amount fingerprinting

Bot shows your TRON wallet address and a unique cent-suffixed amount (e.g. 7.013 USDT — suffix maps to the pending order). A poller watches the address via the free TronGrid API; an incoming transfer matching the fingerprint within 60 min auto-activates. Fallback: user submits tx hash, bot verifies it on-chain.

  • No processor, no MDR — network fee paid by sender
  • 19 confirmations ≈ 1 min on TRON
  • Sweep to cold wallet weekly

08 · The Telegram design

Bot D — the membership bot. Grant on pay, kick on lapse.

One new bot (create via @BotFather, e.g. @BitReinforceXPayBot) plus a small SQLite store. It is the public storefront — anyone can DM it. The signals bot never spams, the control bot stays Pro-only, the membership bot handles money and doors.

The five mechanisms

1
Private channel + join-request gate Signals channel is private. Its standing invite link is created with creates_join_request=True. The membership bot (admin) receives every chat_join_request and approves only if the store shows an active signals subscription for that user id. Shared/leaked links are worthless — the gate is the database, not the link.
2
Paid → personal join-request link On payment the bot mints createChatInviteLink(creates_join_request=True, expire_date=+48h) and DMs it. Every join — even via a personal link — passes the database gate, so a stale link in an old DM can never re-admit a revoked or lapsed member (a member_limit link would admit the first taker unchecked).
3
Auto-kick = ban + immediate unban Expiry job runs hourly: for each lapsed member, ban_chat_member(channel, uid) removes them, then unban_chat_member immediately lifts the ban so they can rejoin the moment they repay. Telegram has no "remove" API — ban+unban IS the kick idiom.
4
Pro gating in the control bot Bot B's six command handlers get wrapped: no active pro row → polite refusal + deep link to /plans. Plus a per-day query counter for the 30/day fair-use cap. This is why Pro can be sold independently — it never touches channel membership.
5
Owner override commands /grant <uid> <days> <sku>, /revoke <uid>, /subs (MRR, active count, expiring-this-week) — admin-id-locked, for comps, refunds and support.

Data model (new subscriptions.db — separate from trading DB)

The Pro gate, concretely

def requires_pro(handler):
    async def wrapped(update, context):
        uid = update.effective_user.id
        if not subs.is_active(uid, "pro"):
            await update.message.reply_text(
                "Pro plan required — tap to subscribe: t.me/BitReinforceXPayBot?start=pro")
            return
        if subs.bump_usage(uid) > 30:
            await update.message.reply_text("Daily fair-use reached (30) — resets 00:00 IST.")
            return
        return await handler(update, context)
    return wrapped

10 · Compliance & honesty

Advisory only. Say it everywhere, mean it.

Not legal advice — spend one consult with a CA before charging money. Summary of the landscape as of mid-2026, verify before launch:
AreaPosition
SEBIInvestment-adviser registration governs securities. Crypto/VDAs are not SEBI-regulated securities — but never use the words "guaranteed", "assured returns", or "portfolio management". "Educational/informational market analysis" is the correct framing, in T&C and in every signal footer.
User-side taxSubscribers' own trading is taxed as VDA income (30% + 1% TDS). Their problem, not yours — but a one-line FAQ note builds trust.
Your incomeSubscription revenue is ordinary business income (INR and USDT both — USDT receipts are taxable at INR value on receipt). GST registration mandatory past ₹20L annual turnover; consider registering early for clean invoicing.
Refund policyPublish one: e.g. full refund within 24h of first purchase if no signals viewed; none after. Razorpay disputes go smoother with a published policy.
Every signalFooter, always: "Educational market analysis, not financial advice. Crypto is high-risk; trade at your own discretion. Past performance ≠ future results."
